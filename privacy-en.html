We collect only what is necessary for the service you come to use. If you simply browse the website, we do not ask you for your name or phone number at all.

2.2 When you become a member of the shop

Membership registration is done at the shop. We record your full name, phone number, and the membership number issued to you.

2.3 When you link your LINE account to a membership number

If you choose to link your LINE account to your membership number, we record your LINE user ID (LINE User ID) together with your membership number, so that the system knows which member is messaging us.
We do not have access to your friends list, your chats with other people, or any other data in your LINE account.

2.4 When you join the shop's Discord community

When you verify yourself with your membership number in our Discord server, the system records your Discord user ID and the membership number used for verification, in order to grant access to the members' rooms.

2.5 When you book the practice room or enroll in a course

We record your name, contact details, the date and time of the booking, and the items you select, in order to schedule and get back to you.

If you book the practice room through the website, we record your name, contact method (phone number and/or email), the date and time of the booking, the equipment setup you selected, any note you type, the language you used, and the channel that brought you to the booking page (for example Instagram or Google Maps), together with the time you ticked the consent box, in order to confirm the booking, get back to you, and let you check the status of your booking with the reference code.

If you open the booking page inside the LINE app, we also record your LINE user ID, in order to send a confirmation back to you through LINE (this can only be sent once you are a friend of the shop's LINE account).

2.6 Photos and videos in the shop or in class

We may take photos or videos of the atmosphere in the shop, in classes, or at events, to publish on the shop's channels. If your face is clearly visible in a photo, we will ask your permission before publishing it, and if you later do not wish it to stay published, tell us at any time and we will take it down.

2.7 Teachers and member data

The shop's teachers are independent contractors, not permanent employees of the shop. Teachers sign in to the shop's back-office system to run lessons, and can access data only within the scope and instructions the shop sets, as listed below. Teachers who also hold a team-member account access data according to that team role (see section 9).

  • Looking up a customer or member by phone number — a teacher types the full phone number to see whether it belongs to a customer or member of the shop. The system shows only the name and the phone number masked down to its last 4 digits (results include students who are minors). Searching by name is not possible, and a teacher cannot create a new customer record. This is used only to enrol students in courses and arrange teaching schedules.
  • Students enrolled with that teacher — the teacher sees the contact details and full phone number of non-minor students who enrol with them, in order to arrange lessons. For students who are minors, the teacher does not see the child's contact details but sees the parent or guardian's name and contact details instead.
  • Calendar appointments — the system pulls appointments from the shop's Google Calendar and links them to class sessions. An appointment title may contain a student's name typed in by the teacher, and a teacher sees only their own appointments.
  • Payments and slips — recording a course student's payment, and attaching and viewing payment slips, can be done by the owner and administrators. General team members can attach a slip to a payment that has already been recorded, but cannot open the slip or see the amount. A teacher can view the amount and slip only for students enrolled with that teacher. (Recording payments and back-dated entries remain limited to the owner and administrators.)

Payment slips are stored as files in a private file store in the system (jpg, png, webp or pdf, up to 5 MB each). The link used to open a slip each time is valid for 60 seconds, and slips are not publicly accessible.

The content on each teacher's profile page (biography, achievements, links and images) is information that each teacher has chosen to publish themselves.

2.8 When you sign in as a member on the website

When you sign in as a member at djlabsiam.com/member, the system uses the member code and phone number the shop already holds from your membership sign-up only to identify which member you are (this is not the sign-in itself), before sending a 6-digit verification code to the e-mail the shop has on file. After the first successful verification you set your own password; the shop cannot see it (it is stored as a hash by Supabase Auth), and the verified e-mail is recorded as the web account's e-mail (used to send a code if you forget the password). The system does not say where the code was sent or whether a member was found.

The code is valid for 10 minutes, can be used once, and is stored in the database as a hash (never the actual digits). Five wrong codes or five wrong passwords lock sign-in temporarily for 15 minutes. This page does not use the Meta Pixel and does not show the pop-up.

After you sign in, the website keeps a sign-in token in your browser (it does not keep your password, phone number or member code) and removes it when you press sign out. The page shows only your name, member code, whether LINE is linked and, if you have joined, your nickname and rank on the game leaderboard.

If you choose to link LINE (optional), the website loads LINE's script and takes you to LINE's sign-in page. The shop sends the token LINE issues to LINE for checking, then stores your LINE user ID and the time of linking with your member record (no LINE name or profile picture). Once linked you cannot change it yourself; staff must do it. If you do not press link, the shop stores nothing from LINE.

6. How long we keep data

  • Member data — kept for as long as you are a member, and deleted when you ask us to delete it
  • Web member sign-in data — verification codes expired for more than 1 day and the guess-limiting counters (stored as hashes) are deleted when the clean-up is run · security event records (time, event type, success or not; no phone, e-mail, name, IP or password) are kept for 90 days · the verified e-mail and sign-in account are kept until the member is deleted or staff remove the account
  • Game leaderboard data — the nickname and best result per level are kept until you press "Hide from the board" or "Delete all my scores", or the member is deleted · the order of cable plugging is not stored · request counters (hashes) are deleted when the clean-up in the item above runs
  • Sales documents and warranty certificates — kept for the period required by accounting and tax law
  • Course students' payment slips — kept for 12 months after the course ends, after which the shop owner clears them manually on a periodic basis (there is no automatic deletion yet). Clearing applies to the slip file only; the payment record itself (date, amount and payment method) remains. If an administrator deletes a payment record that has a slip attached, the slip file may remain in the file store until the owner deletes it.
  • Practice room booking data for bookings made through the website or LINE — kept for 12 months after the booking date; after that we delete the data that identifies you (name, contact details, LINE user ID), while the date, time, and amount are kept for accounting
  • Other booking data — kept only as long as necessary to provide the service and for later review
  • LINE and Discord user IDs — deleted when you unlink your account or leave the community

If you want to know how long your own data in particular is kept, contact us through the channels in section 12. We will answer according to your case.

7. Your rights

Under the PDPA you have the following rights, and you can exercise them free of charge.

  • Access — ask to see what data we hold about you, and ask for a copy
  • Rectification — have your data kept accurate and up to date
  • Erasure — have data deleted when it no longer needs to be kept
  • Restriction — ask us to stop using your data temporarily
  • Objection — object to your data being used for marketing or other reasons
  • Data portability — ask to receive your data in a format you can reuse
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw at any time, without affecting what was lawfully done before
  • Complain — complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee Office if you believe we have acted improperly

Contact us through the channels in section 12. We will act within the time the law requires.

8. Cookies, Meta Pixel and turning off tracking

This website does not create cookies of its own, but it uses the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms to measure the shop's advertising on Facebook and Instagram — how many people the ads bring to look at the courses or contact the shop. The Meta Pixel starts as soon as you open a page, and sets Meta cookies named _fbp (and _fbc, if you arrived from a Facebook or Instagram ad) in your browser. These cookies last about 3 months. The practice room booking page (djlabsiam.com/book) and the member sign-in page (djlabsiam.com/member) do not use the Meta Pixel.

8.1 What the Meta Pixel sends to Meta

  • Sent — which page of the website you open (that page's address and the page you came from), whether you open the courses page or a teacher's profile, and whether you press the shop's LINE link or phone number (only that it was LINE or phone — the number and any message are not sent), together with technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the device you use, and the ID in the _fbp cookie
  • Not sent — your name, phone number, email, anything you type, or booking details; and we have turned off the Meta Pixel's advanced matching
  • If you are logged in to Facebook or Instagram in the same browser, Meta may link this visit to your account, in line with Meta's policy
  • Meta stores and uses the data it receives under Meta's own privacy policy, and the data may be sent and stored outside Thailand, as described in section 5

8.2 How to turn it off

  • Press "Turn off ad measurement" at the bottom of this page, or of any other page of the website that uses the Meta Pixel — the website will no longer load the Meta Pixel in this browser, and deletes the _fbp / _fbc cookies it set. Your choice is remembered in your browser (if you clear your browser's site data you will need to press it again), and you can press the same button again to turn it back on.
  • If your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control (GPC) switched on, we treat that as already off and do not load the Meta Pixel
  • Set your browser to block third-party cookies, or use a browser extension that blocks tracking scripts
  • Adjust the ad settings in your own Facebook or Instagram account

If you press "Play here" on the video page, YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com) may keep data in your browser under Google's policy; if you press play on the podcast episode in the pop-up (the Spotify player), Spotify may do so under Spotify's policy; if you press play on a Facebook video in the pop-up, Meta may do so under Meta's policy; and if you use AR on Android through Google Scene Viewer, processing inside the Google app follows Google's policy. The button above does not cover these. Use the browser setting or extension described above.
